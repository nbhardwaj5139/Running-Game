# KITSUNE — 狐 · a run across Japan

A 3D endless runner. A fox spirit runs the old road from Kyoto to Okinawa while a typhoon chases it. Shrine stairs, neon cities, rice paddies, coast roads, four seasons, kaiju, and twelve spirit powers. Runs in any modern browser, nothing to install.

## Quick start

1. Open the link above (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari on a laptop or desktop).
2. Pick your character. In 2-player mode pick one for each player.
3. Pick a difficulty: Easy, Normal or Hard.
4. Tick **God mode** if you just want to see the whole journey without dying.
5. Choose **1 PLAYER** or **2 PLAYERS**, or press `1` / `2`.

The road is the same six lanes whether one or two people are running.

## Controls

| Action | Player 1 (right road) | Player 2 (left road) |
|---|---|---|
| Change lane | `←` / `→` | `A` / `D` |
| Jump (hold for a higher jump) | `↑` or `Space` | `W` |
| Slide / fast-fall in the air | `↓` | `S` |
| Pause / resume | `Esc` or `P` | |
| Restart | `R` | |

In 1-player mode both the arrows and WASD drive the fox. On a touchscreen, swipe: left and right to change lane, up to jump, down to slide, tap to jump.

In 2-player mode you share one keyboard. Either runner can cross onto the other half of the road and barge the other one sideways. One typhoon chases both of you.

## How to survive

- **The typhoon bar** at the bottom is your health. Every stumble, hit post or fall lets the storm gain ground. Running clean and collecting coins pushes it back. When the bar is full the storm takes you.
- After the first stretch the storm also pushes forward on its own, faster in thunderstorms and blizzards and much faster while an avalanche is chasing you. That is why the bar drifts even when you run clean.
- Every obstacle has one answer, and the game tells you after a hit:
  - **Posts, statues, vending machines, poles:** change lane.
  - **Low blocks, logs, boulders, barriers:** jump.
  - **Gates, curtains, banners, ropes:** slide.
  - **Holes in the road:** jump.
  - **Wide wrecks (trucks, boats, fallen cedars):** take the one free lane.
  - **Rollers (barrels, buoys, a Shiba, a salaryman on a bike):** they sweep between two lanes, take the one they never visit or time it.
  - **Shockwaves thrown by a kaiju:** jump, they span the whole road.
- A near miss beside a post or hole gives bonus points.
- Coins (koban) add score and push the storm back a little. Coins floating high need a jump.

## The journey

The road cycles through provinces, each a long stretch of its own scenery. Seasons turn every two provinces.

1. **Kyoto** — cedar forest and bamboo groves, then the shrine stairs: a torii tunnel climbing to a shrine at the top and back down.
2. **Osaka** — neon streets, pachinko signs, vending machines, the shinkansen on its viaduct.
3. **Nara** — quiet suburbs and rice paddies with deer that roam the verges and bow as you pass.
4. **Shōnan** — the coast road: cliffs, tetrapods, fishing boats, gulls, the sea on your right.
5. **Hakone** — mountain road under Mount Fuji, with its own shrine climb.
6. **Tokyo** — the big city at night, wet asphalt reflecting the billboards.
7. **Hokkaido** — always under snow.
8. **Okinawa** — turquoise water and a torii standing in the sea.

Spring brings cherry petals, summer fireflies and festival lanterns, autumn red leaves that lie on the road and scatter when you run through them, winter snow on everything.

**Weather** changes per stretch and changes the rules: rain and snow make lane changes slower, high wind and thunderstorms shove you a lane sideways (watch for the gust arrow), fog hides what is coming.

**Kaiju** rise beside the road at the end of every season and throw their hazards onto it: the mountain giant Daidarabotchi, the sea giant Umibōzu, the skeleton Gashadokuro and the snow ogre Yuki-Oni.

**Set pieces:** a wooden bridge that gives way under you as you cross it, and an avalanche that chases you down the winter shrine stairs.

## Tips

- Hold jump for a long jump, tap it for a short hop. Press slide in the air to drop fast.
- The camera looks into turns before you reach them. Watch the road ahead, not the fox.
- Jetpack is the fastest thing in the game. Ground hazards cannot touch you while flying.
- In 2-player mode a well-timed barge can push your rival into a post.
- The seed is the date, so everyone runs the same road on the same day. Add `?seed=anything` to the link for a different road.
- If it runs slowly, the game lowers its render resolution on its own. Closing other tabs helps.
